`SummaryBar` takes an optional `detail`, a second, smaller line under the summary (e.g. the time and the price), so the part a parent checks is not the part an ellipsis eats on a phone; the bar keeps its fixed height. `hideNextWhenDisabled` draws «next» only while it does something and puts the step's hint in its place (opt-in keys `summary.hint.who`, `summary.hint.service`, `summary.hint.when`), for steps that move on by themselves once answered. New class-name slots `detail` and `hint`.
