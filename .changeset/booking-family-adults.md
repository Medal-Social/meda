`WhoScreen`: a logged-in parent's list can count its grown-ups too. With `guestParty.adults` and the new optional `who.family.adults` label, the «myself» check row becomes the «Voksne» stepper — «myself» on seat 1, the guest's seats 2 and 3 after it — so a second grown-up a guest picked survives a login, and a parent can bring one. Without the label the check row stays.
