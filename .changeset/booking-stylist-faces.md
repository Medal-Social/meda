`StylistScreen` takes `firstAvailableFaces`: «first available» is drawn as the faces of the first three stylists with a photo who can do the whole basket, instead of its badge, falling back to the badge with fewer than two photos. `edgeFade` fades the right edge of the phone's sideways row, so a cut-off card reads as «more this way». `StylistOption` has an optional `faces`. Both are off unless passed.
